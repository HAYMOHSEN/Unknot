Privacy policy

Unknot – Stress Relief

Effective 10 October 2026 · Published by Jotech

The short version

Unknot does not collect, send or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your stress check-ins, session ratings, parked worries, journal entries, next steps and settings are saved in your device's local app storage.
  • This data never leaves your device. There is no account, no server and no cloud sync.
  • You can export your data (CSV or a backup file) or delete all of it at any time in Settings → Your data. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone.
  • The app works offline. It does not need an internet connection after installation.

Notifications

If you turn on break reminders and allow notifications, Unknot shows them locally on your device. No notification data is sent anywhere.

Health information

Unknot offers self-help techniques for everyday stress. It does not diagnose or treat any medical or mental-health condition and is not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number or find a helpline at findahelpline.com.

Children

Unknot is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
